The 5x8 bitmap font in stheme.py is the classic font of the Adafruit GFX
Library (glcdfont.c, https://github.com/adafruit/Adafruit-GFX-Library), the
same font the firmware draws through GFX Library for Arduino. It was copied
from SmallTV Studio's src/core/font.ts. Its licence follows.
